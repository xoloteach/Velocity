# Project Velocity — Neon 4 Improvement Plan

Execution plan for elevating the existing Godot 4.6.3 open-world speedster action RPG on local branch `neon4-improvements` without rebuilding from scratch or breaking any public API, save schema, or test contract.

## Architecture Principles
- Preserve all core systems (`world`, `population`, `combat`, `powers`, `progress`, `rewind`, `campaign`, `presentation`, `hud`) and their deterministic snapshot/restore and save-ledger guarantees.
- Keep Godot 4.6.3 Compatibility renderer (WebGL 2 single-thread) as the sole runtime engine.
- Upgrade 3D geometry, rigging/skinning, distinct boss silhouettes, city architecture, grounded shadows, VFX, audio, HUD polish, and performance scalability sequentially across 18 verified checkpoints.
- Credit all assets honestly in `ASSET_CREDITS.md` (100% original/procedural or verified CC0/MIT assets; zero pirated or unlicensed IP).

## Sequential Checkpoints
1. **CP01 — Baseline Audit & Visual Capture**: Clone repo, branch locally, run native tests, export baseline Web build, capture Chromium visuals, write `docs/NEON4_AUDIT.md`, `PLAN.md`, `CHECKPOINTS.md`.
2. **CP02 — Stability, Cleanup & Leak Fixes**: Fix `ObjectDB instances leaked at exit`, tighten null/bounds/import hygiene, verify zero regression in `test_native.sh`.
3. **CP03 — Hero Model, Smooth Skinning, Proportions & Distinct Boss/Enemy Rigs**: Build upgraded smooth-skinned multi-bone `velocity-runner.glb` (athletic superhero proportions, helmet ear-fins, glowing speed conduits, sculpted armor) PLUS bespoke distinct silhouette GLBs for **Nullstride** (`nullstride-boss.glb`) and **Afterlight** (`afterlight-boss.glb`) and tactical enemy attachments (`guard`, `speedster`), plus grounded contact shadows.
4. **CP04 — Grounded Locomotion & Combat Animations**: Upgrade all 29 skeletal animation clips with natural counter-rotation, heel-toe stride arcs, heroic idle breathing, aerodynamic sprint/boost lean, 3-point landing crouch, wall-cant banking, and punchy combat/power poses; add procedural acceleration lean & turn bank in `game.gd`.
5. **CP05 — Movement, Speed Fantasy & Camera Polish**: Add multi-strand arcing body lightning at high speed, radial camera/HUD speed-lines at Mach+/Extreme Transit, wall-run sparks, water-run wakes, smooth shoulder framing & camera collision damping.
6. **CP06 — Landscape Touchscreen HUD & Controller Polish**: Tighten top operation banner, add live Rewind history gauge, clear physical speed (km/h & Mach) vs Speed Force tier readout, refined superhero card styling, left/right-handed touch presets, Web audio tap-to-start unlock banner, and crisp ability wheel/editor feedback.
7. **CP07 — City Architecture, Grounding & Atmospheric Lighting**: Add multi-tier architectural setbacks, rooftop mechanical/antenna/spire variety, richer facade depth/reflection shader, upgraded branching trees & street furniture (hydrants, bus stops, bollards, overhanging signals), contact shadows under props/structures, optional directional sunlight shadows on High/Ultra, and balanced day-night cycle.
8. **CP08 — Traffic, Crowds, Police & Crime Polish**: Upgrade pedestrians to anatomically proportioned multi-segment humans with varied silhouettes/clothing/accessories & contact shadows; upgrade vehicles to detailed sedan, coupe, taxi, SUV, van, and police interceptor with pillars, grilles, mirrors, rotating wheels, light pools, and contact shadows.
9. **CP09 — Speed Force Powers VFX & Feedback**: Upgrade all 9 powers with layered lightning core+halo+sparks, hero phase translucency shader, multi-ring swirling tornado funnel, glowing slow-time atmospheric vignette, expressive mirage clone trail, swirling Rift portal vortex, and crisp shockwave/redirect impact rings.
10. **CP10 — Rewind Visual, Audio & HUD Polish**: Add reverse motion ghost/trail playback, temporal screen vignette/scanlines, reverse audio sweep, and live HUD rewind buffer indicator while preserving exact multi-entity determinism and anti-duplication ledger.
11. **CP11 — Combat Polish**: Upgrade 3D attack telegraphs (animated danger lanes, expanding hazard rings, directional lock-on reticle), hit-spark bursts, directional knockback, and readable counter/opening flashes.
12. **CP12 — Boss Encounters (Nullstride & Afterlight)**: Integrate bespoke `nullstride-boss.glb` and `afterlight-boss.glb` rigs with phase-shift shockwaves, aura lightning, distinct attack trails, and fair readable openings verified via real-command wins.
13. **CP13 — 8-Mission Campaign & Postgame Polish**: Add tall vertical 3D objective beacons/pillars, polished mission banners and subtitles, checkpoint resume clarity, ending celebration, and postgame races/patrol/replay verification.
14. **CP14 — Economy, Gacha & Progression Polish**: Polish Workshop & Rift Cache UI, rarity reveal glow/fanfare + instant skip, material exchange button in Inventory/Cache, and offline durability verification.
15. **CP15 — Upgraded Audio & Dynamic Score**: Rebuild audio generator (`tools/create_audio.py`) at 32 kHz with rich multi-layered synth/filtered-noise SFX, heroic multi-part city music theme, dynamic boss combat stem, Rift ambience, reverse rewind sweep, and UI/power cues.
16. **CP16 — Performance Optimization & 4-Preset Benchmarking**: Tune Low/Medium/High/Ultra visual budgets (render scale, shadows, draw distance, FX density, crowd caps) and run deterministic benchmark across all 4 presets.
17. **CP17 — Full End-to-End Native & Browser QA Verification**: Run all native tests + new regression checks, export final Godot Web build, verify full gameplay/HUD/touch/rewind/gacha/bosses/campaign and visual screenshots in Chromium, critique & fix top defects.
18. **CP18 — Port-3000 Web Host + Final Delivery Packaging**: Serve final Godot Web export cleanly on port 3000, package `project-velocity-final-web.zip` and `project-velocity-final-source.zip`, verify archive extraction & boot, and deliver final documentation (`FINAL_QA_REPORT.md`, `ASSET_CREDITS.md`, `README.md`, `RELEASE_NOTES.md`).
